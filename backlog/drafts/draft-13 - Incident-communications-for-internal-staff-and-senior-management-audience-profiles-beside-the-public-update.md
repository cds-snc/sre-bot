## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Optional enhancement, not scheduled; needs the communications and policy roles to adopt it. The comms subdomain publishes public status updates with one audience profile. This draft adds two profiles over the same form, approval and history: an internal update (a severity-and-summary title, a one- or two-sentence state, current status bullets, next steps, when the next update comes, with a completeness checklist), and a senior-management brief (the same structure without technical terms, for incidents that involve external stakeholders). Each is a comms profile selecting labels, layout and the fields shown; nothing posts automatically. A status-page publisher adapter for the organisation's status page is a sibling draft.
