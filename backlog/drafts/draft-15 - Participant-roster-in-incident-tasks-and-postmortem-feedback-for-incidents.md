## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Optional enhancement, not scheduled; reference only, taken from other incident tools. Three records under the incident that the current process does not ask for: a participant roster (everyone who joined the conversation, with their role if any, kept current by a job so the postmortem knows who was involved), in-incident tasks (owner, due time, done, opened from the conversation), and a short feedback form sent to participants when the incident closes. Participants would live in core (postmortem and comms read them), tasks in response, feedback in postmortem. Revisit when the process owners ask for any of the three.
