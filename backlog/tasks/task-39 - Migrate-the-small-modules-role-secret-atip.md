## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Rescoped 2026-09-24 to decisions/plugin-architecture.md and migration.md: legacy modules are rebuilt by surface, not moved. These are the small wins that cement the pattern. One module per PR series, in the order role, secret, atip.

Per module:
1. Confirm each surface's smoke coverage and target in the TASK-36 inventory.
2. Rebuild in app/features/<feature>/ in the layout-table shape, generated by the package generator (TASK-114):
   - handlers in entrypoints/slack.py, registered through the Slack handler contract (TASK-26.1);
   - strings through the translator contract with EN and FR catalogues (TASK-118; the python-i18n and app/locales/ usage goes away);
   - settings values in the TOML files (TASK-111), with the old app/infrastructure/configuration/features/<name>.py slice deleted;
   - an entry point and an enablement key.
3. Cut over: remove the module from _register_legacy_handlers(), delete app/modules/<name>/, keep smoke green before and after.

Targets:
- role -> features/talent/ (moved there first by TASK-124.4). decisions/workplace-systems.md makes talent-role records a record of truth in app storage, not a Google Sheet or Doc, so the rebuilt role surface stores its records through the storage contract. Any surviving sheet or doc is a collaboration space or a write-only projection.
- secret -> features/secret/.
- atip -> features/atip/. Its Trello card operations arrive in its adapters/ from TASK-25.9.
