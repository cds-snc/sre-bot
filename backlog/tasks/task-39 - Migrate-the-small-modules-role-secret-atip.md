## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
decisions/plugin-architecture.md and migration.md: legacy modules are rebuilt by surface, not moved. These are the small wins that cement the pattern. One module per PR series, in the order role, secret, atip.

Per module:
1. Confirm each surface's pin and target in the TASK-36 inventory (pinned by TASK-36.1.3).
2. Rebuild in app/features/<feature>/ in the layout-table shape, generated by the package generator (TASK-114):
   - handlers in entrypoints/slack.py, registered through the Slack handler contract (TASK-26.1);
   - strings through the translator contract with EN and FR catalogues (TASK-118; the python-i18n and app/locales/ usage goes away);
   - a settings slice in the feature's settings.py with its values in the configuration files (decisions/configuration.md); nothing reads app/infrastructure/configuration/ (TASK-111.14 deletes the dead AtipSettings slice);
   - an entry point and an enablement key.
3. Cut over: remove the module from _register_legacy_handlers() (server/lifespan.py:93), delete app/modules/<name>/, keep the pins green before and after.

Targets:
- role -> features/talent/ (moved there first by TASK-124.4). decisions/workplace-systems.md makes talent-role records a record of truth owned by the app, not a Google Sheet or Doc, so the rebuilt role surface stores its records through the records contract. Any surviving sheet or doc is a collaboration space or a write-only projection.
- secret -> features/secret/.
- atip -> features/atip/. Its Trello card operations arrive in its adapters/ from TASK-25.9, and the ATIP board id (trello_atip_board, today in integrations/trello/settings.py) moves into the atip slice's table.
