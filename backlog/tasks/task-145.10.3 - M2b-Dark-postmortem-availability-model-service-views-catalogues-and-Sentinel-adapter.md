## 2. Decisions (each judged against M3, M4a, M4b, TASK-192, TASK-147, TASK-145.13)
D1 Two attendee sets. Required ("Must attend") defaults to the incident's role holders (incident_commander, operations_lead, postmortem_owner; de-duplicated), or the opener when none is set. Optional ("Invite") defaults to the conversation's other human, active members. Both are Slack multi_users_select pickers (any workspace user may be added, e.g. a manager outside the channel), replacing the legacy static member list (pin :136 is edited in M3 under NC9).
D2 Availability per attendee: FREE, BUSY, UNKNOWN (AttendeeBusy.busy None, or no email). Unknown is never free.
D3 Slot rule: a candidate slot qualifies when no required attendee is BUSY at it (a required UNKNOWN is allowed and named) and the optional quorum holds: free optional / counted optional >= optional_quorum_percent, where unknown optional count as not free and an optional attendee busy at every candidate in the search is excluded from the count and named; no counted optional = quorum holds. A required attendee busy at every candidate stops the search (reason names them). The earliest qualifying slot wins. Explanations: required unknown, optional busy or unknown at the chosen slot, excluded optional, and when no slot: the reason (required always busy, quorum never met, no candidate) with names.
D4 Time (NC7, kind (a)): candidates are built per local date in the meeting zone with zoneinfo (window start/end as local times combined with each date, so DST moves the UTC hour), weekdays from a setting, holidays for every year the search crosses, slots stepping by slot_step_minutes, ending by the window end, starting after now (injected clock) plus earliest_offset_days. Default zone America/Toronto (identical rules to the legacy US/Eastern for this audience; no visible change); the zone abbreviation shown is computed per date (EST/EDT), fixing the legacy fixed "EDT".
D5 Holiday failure policy is configuration (human 2026-11-25: no hard-coded policy): [incident_postmortem] holiday_lookup_failure = "proceed" | "refuse", default "proceed" in base.toml. proceed: the search runs without holidays, logs holiday_lookup_failed at WARNING (observability.md: degraded-but-handled) with error_code, and the result carries holidays_unavailable so the view and the channel notice say public holidays could not be checked. refuse: the schedule fails with a classified error and a localized reason. Grounding: reliability.md takes a read-failure policy per use, explicitly (facades' FAIL_CLOSED is a required argument); outbound-clients.md leaves the meaning of a classified failure to the caller; the holiday list is a soft dependency (AWS Well-Architected REL05-BP01, graceful degradation); legacy proceeded silently (errors swallowed to []).
